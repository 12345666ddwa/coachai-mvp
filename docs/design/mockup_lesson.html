Data collection and quality
Year 12 · Data science · 60 min · Draft · teacher edits before use

Learning objectives

  • Distinguish quantitative from qualitative data and match data types to each
  • Select an appropriate sampling method for a given enterprise scenario
  • Identify the legal obligations that apply when collecting student-adjacent data

Lesson flow

0–10 min
Hook: the messiest survey
Show a deliberately flawed survey. Students list everything wrong with it, leading into quality categories.
10–30 min
Direct instruction + worked example
Quantitative vs qualitative; data types; levels of measurement. Worked example using the class's own attendance data.
30–50 min
Group task: sampling choice
Each group receives an enterprise scenario and defends a sampling method (active vs passive, manual vs computerised).
50–60 min
Debrief + exit ticket
One question: which legal issue is most likely to apply to your scenario, and why?
Assessment point Exit ticket sorted into: needs reteach / on track / ready for extension. Feeds directly into the next lesson's groupings.
Syllabus alignment
Explore the difference between quantitative and qualitative data
Investigate data sampling, including manual and computerised methods
Investigate the legal issues surrounding data collection and handling
