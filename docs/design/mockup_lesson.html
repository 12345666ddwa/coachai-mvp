Data collection and quality 数据的收集与质量
Year 12 · Data science · 60 min · Draft · teacher edits before use 12 年级 · Data science · 60 分钟 · 草稿 · 教师修改后使用

Learning objectives 学习目标

  • Distinguish quantitative from qualitative data and match data types to each 区分定量与定性数据，并为它们匹配对应的数据类型
  • Select an appropriate sampling method for a given enterprise scenario 针对给定的企业场景选择合适的抽样方法
  • Identify the legal obligations that apply when collecting student-adjacent data 识别收集与学生相关数据时适用的法律义务

Lesson flow 课堂流程

0–10
Hook: the messiest survey 导入：最糟糕的问卷
Show a deliberately flawed survey. Students list everything wrong with it, leading into quality categories. 展示一份故意设计得漏洞百出的问卷，让学生找出所有问题，引出数据质量分类。
10–30
Direct instruction + worked example 讲解与例题
Quantitative vs qualitative; data types; levels of measurement. Worked example using the class's own attendance data. 定量与定性、数据类型、测量层级；用班级自己的出勤数据做例题。
30–50
Group task: sampling choice 小组任务：选择抽样方法
Each group receives an enterprise scenario and defends a sampling method (active vs passive, manual vs computerised). 每组拿到一个企业场景，论证其抽样方法的选择（主动/被动、人工/计算机化）。
50–60
Debrief + exit ticket 总结与出门条
One question: which legal issue is most likely to apply to your scenario, and why? 一个问题：你的场景最可能涉及哪项法律问题？为什么？
Assessment point 评估点 Exit ticket sorted into: needs reteach / on track / ready for extension. Feeds directly into the next lesson's groupings. 出门条分三类：需重讲 / 已掌握 / 可拓展，直接用于下一节课的分组。
Syllabus alignment 大纲对齐
Explore the difference between quantitative and qualitative data 辨析定量数据与定性数据的区别
Investigate data sampling, including manual and computerised methods 研究数据抽样，包括人工与计算机化方法
Investigate the legal issues surrounding data collection and handling 研究数据收集与处理相关的法律问题
